## Research-application paragraph

In CampusBank, I explored how a conversational interface can connect an information request to a service action and then to human support while retaining context. I iteratively defined the interaction requirements and developed the prototype with AI coding assistance, combining bilingual DeepSeek dialogue, grounded tool use, persistent conversations, guided service windows, and role-separated customer and staff workflows. The resulting system includes reproducible behavior checks and a hosted demonstration. It provides a practical platform for my interest in human–AI interaction, particularly multilingual grounding, responsibility cues, and continuity across AI-to-human handoff. Formal user studies and comparative experiments remain future work.
